Radio Wilma is a free internet radio player, a companion to the Radio Wilma 4.0 ESP32-S3 radio.

• Radio-style screen: clock and date, weather, station name, artist and song title, vinyl, equalizer, volume. Portrait and landscape.
• Favourite stations, search in the radio-browser.info directory and adding a station by URL.
• Station logos: upload your own or download from the directory.
• Background playback, a lock-screen tile with previous/next station, pause and close buttons.
• Five colour themes and a sleep timer.

No account, no ads, no data collection. Permissions: internet, notifications and a background playback service.
